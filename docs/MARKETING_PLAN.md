# DentalFlow AI — Marketing Plan & Clinic Survey

Positioning, launch sequence, channel plan, and the validation survey with
pre-committed decision rules. Companion to [FIRST_CUSTOMER.md](./FIRST_CUSTOMER.md)
and [DEMO_SCRIPT.md](./DEMO_SCRIPT.md).

---

## 1. Positioning

**Category:** Missed-Patient Recovery System (not "AI chatbot" — chatbots are a
₹999 commodity).

**Pitch:** *"Clinics lose 30–40% of WhatsApp inquiries after hours. DentalFlow
answers every message in seconds, books the appointment, follows up with
everyone who didn't book — and shows you the recovered revenue every Monday."*

**Price:** ₹2,499/mo (₹1,499/mo billed annually). Pro ₹4,999. 14-day free
trial, free white-glove setup. One recovered patient ≈ a year of subscription.

**Golden rule:** the receptionist is the *hero*, never the casualty. She gets
superpowers (after-hours coverage, no repeat questions, one dashboard); the
owner gets the Monday report.

## 2. Launch sequence

1. **Design partners (weeks 1–3):** 5 clinics at ₹999 locked-for-life for
   feedback + testimonial + case-study rights. Source via the survey below.
2. **Proof (week 4):** one case study — real Monday-report screenshots — cut
   into a 60-second WhatsApp-forwardable video.
3. **Scale (month 2+):** channels in order of leverage:
   - **Dental supplier / lab reps** (in 20 clinics a week): ₹1,000 per closed referral
   - **Local IDA chapter meetings:** live 10-minute demos
   - **Dentist Instagram micro-influencers:** 2–3 paid collabs
   - **Referral loop:** 1 month free per referred clinic
   - Walk-ins are the *last* channel, not the first.

**Budget:** ₹20–30k/month. **Funnel target:** 400 contacts → 80 demos (20%) →
20 paying (25%) by month 6 ≈ ₹50k MRR.

## 3. The survey — "Dental Clinic WhatsApp Audit"

Frame as a free audit, not research. Incentive: a free response-time audit +
first month free for pilot clinics. Distribute: Google Form via supplier reps,
IDA WhatsApp groups, and 20 in-person visits. Target **n ≥ 30**.

1. How do new patients most often first contact you? *(Call / WhatsApp / Walk-in / Instagram / Practo)*
2. Roughly how many WhatsApp inquiries per week? *(<10 / 10–30 / 30–60 / 60+)*
3. Who replies to them? *(Receptionist / Doctor personally / Whoever's free / Often nobody)*
4. What happens to messages after 8 PM? *(Answered same night / Next morning / Honestly, often missed)*
5. How many inquiries per month never become bookings? *(0–2 / 3–5 / 6–10 / 10+ / No idea)*
6. What is one new patient worth to you, roughly? *(<₹2k / ₹2–10k / ₹10–25k / ₹25k+)*
7. How do you send appointment reminders today? *(Manually on WhatsApp / Calls / Software / We don't)*
8. No-shows per week? *(0–1 / 2–4 / 5+)*
9. Would you let an AI assistant reply instantly and book on your clinic's WhatsApp? *(Yes / Yes, if my staff can review / No — why?)*
10. Would you want this on your **existing number**, or a **separate dedicated booking number**? *(Existing / Separate / Don't care)*
11. If it visibly recovered 2–3 lost patients a month, what would you pay monthly? *(Nothing / <₹1,000 / ₹1,000–2,500 / ₹2,500–4,000 / More)*
12. Rank what matters most: instant replies · booking · reminders · follow-ups · weekly revenue report
13. Open: your biggest headache in patient communication?
14. Want a free 30-day pilot + your clinic's WhatsApp audit? *(Name / clinic / number)*

## 4. Pre-committed decision rules

| Signal | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Pain (Q4/Q5) | ≥40% admit missing after-hours inquiries | Pain confirmed — proceed |
| Price (Q11) | ≥30% willing to pay ≥₹2,000 | Launch at ₹2,499; if mode is ₹1,000–2,500 → launch ₹1,999 |
| Number strategy (Q10) | Majority "existing number" | Coexistence-capable BSP becomes engineering priority #1 |
| | Majority "separate / don't care" | Ship the cheap dedicated-number path first |
| Pilots (Q14) | ≥5 opt-ins | Your design partners — onboard within a week |
| Response volume | <15 responses in 2 weeks | Distribution is broken — fix channels before touching code |
